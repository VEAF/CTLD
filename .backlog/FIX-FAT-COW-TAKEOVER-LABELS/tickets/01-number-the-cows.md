# 01 — Every AI cow has a number, and the announcements name it

**Status:** ✅ done (PR #317) · **Type:** AFK

## Parent

[PRD — FIX-FAT-COW-TAKEOVER-LABELS](../PRD.md). Stories 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 18 (the announcement texts). GitHub issue #298.

## What to build

Give every AI Fat Cow a stable number, and let the messages that tell a flight about a free cow name it, so that ticket 02 can name the entries of the "Take over a Fat Cow" list the same way.

- **The number.** Every AI cow gets a number when it is registered, drawn from the counter that already numbers the group names of requested cows ("Fat Cow <flight> #N"). A requested cow keeps exactly the number of its group name. A cow placed by the mission (`fatCowAICows`) takes the next number when it is activated, in the order of that list. The number is kept on the cow and never changes. Neither the group name of a requested cow nor the group name a mission maker gave a placed cow changes, and the latter is never shown to pilots.
- **The announcements.** The three messages sent to the flights that could take a cow over (released; lost its flight and going home to a base; lost its flight and going home) name the cow with its number, in the words of the PRD ("Fat Cow #3 lost its flight and is going home to Akrotiri: 12.4 km at 270° from you, with …"). They keep their distance and bearing.
- **The texts.** The three messages are changed texts, each a single literal passed to the translation function. The dictionaries are regenerated by the build; French and Spanish are provided, Korean is written by the assistant and flagged for review in the pull request.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A cow requested by a flight carries the number found in its group name.
- [x] A cow placed by the mission carries the next number from the same counter, and several placed cows are numbered in the order of `fatCowAICows`; the numbers of requested cows created afterwards go on from there, with no number used twice.
- [x] The number of a cow does not change while it exists.
- [x] The group name of a requested cow, and the group name of a placed cow, are unchanged.
- [x] Each of the three announcements names the cow with its number and keeps its distance, bearing and stock.
- [x] The new cases fail before the change (the cows have no number, the messages do not name it) and pass after it; the other Fat Cow specs pass unchanged.
- [x] The dictionaries are regenerated and the new texts have French and Spanish translations; the Korean ones are flagged for review.
- [x] `busted tests/ci/` green; luacheck clean.

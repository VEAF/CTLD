**Status:** ⬜ ready · **Type:** AFK

## Parent

## What to build

- **Docs, English and French:** pilot, mission-maker and developer pages are corrected and
  completed.
  - The Il-76 is AI-only and has no native cargo; the C-130J-30 is the only fixed-wing native-carry
    aircraft.
  - A native-carry vehicle is released by DCS and tracked by CTLD (ground unload, in-flight
    parachute release); the vehicle subsystem page no longer says the exit is a no-op.
  - The two carry modes and their rule: a cargo item is unloaded the way it was loaded.
  - A per-type table of what each aircraft can do natively.
  - `aircraftHoldBox` explained for mission makers, with the measured DCS boxes per type and the
    fact that the DCS box is the model's UserBox, not its bounding box.
- **Code comments** that call the Il-76 native or promise a parachute exit through the old mechanism
  are corrected.
- **Roadmap:** the native bbox-exit entry is closed; two follow-up entries are added — the native
  crate F10 lists (*Drop Crate(s)* and *Parachute Crates* include native-carry crates, against the
  comments and the parachute page) and the vehicle weight limit enforced for the AI only.
- **Changelog:** the `[Unreleased]` entries of the lot are consolidated and consistent.
- **Finalization:** the PRD status is set to done, and the lot's index line in the backlog README
  is set to `merged (PR #NN)` in the delivering PR.

# Pilot guide

This section is for **pilots** — how to operate CTLD from the cockpit through the F10 radio menu:
transporting troops, sling-loading crates, deploying vehicles and JTACs, and more. If you are
building or configuring a mission, see the [Mission Maker guide](../mission-maker/index.md)
instead.

## What CTLD lets you do

CTLD turns a transport helicopter (and some fixed-wing aircraft) into a logistics platform. You
fly to a zone, use the **F10 → CTLD** menu to load or spawn what you need, carry it, and set it
down where it is wanted. Menu entries are context-sensitive — they appear based on your aircraft,
whether you are on the ground or airborne, and whether you are inside a relevant zone.

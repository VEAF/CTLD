# Fat Cow

A Fat Cow is a CH-47 that lands in the field and becomes a forward refuel and rearm point for other helicopters.
The US Army calls it "a rapidly employed FARP": the Chinook carries its fuel in tanks inside the hold and hands it out on the ground.

DCS cannot pass fuel from one aircraft to another, so CTLD lays out a small FARP next to the cow: an invisible helipad, a fuel depot behind the ramp, a few crates and three soldiers standing by.
The FARP's stock is exactly what the cow carries.
When it is gone, it is gone.

There are two ways to get a cow: fly one yourself, or ask CTLD for an AI one.

## Flying a cow (CH-47F)

Everything is in **F10 → CTLD → Fat Cow**.

1. Land in a logistic zone.
2. Request what you want to carry, one crate at a time:
    - **Fat Cow fuel tank (800 gal)**: 2 400 kg of jet fuel, and 2 400 kg in your hold;
    - **Fat Cow Hellfires (x4)**;
    - **Fat Cow 30 mm (x300)**.
3. Load the crates into the hold, like any CTLD crate.
4. Fly to where the attack helicopters need you, and land.
5. **Deploy the Fat Cow FARP.**
   The FARP opens a few seconds later and you are told what it holds.
   The crates stay in your hold: the fuel never leaves the Chinook.
6. The other helicopters land next to you and rearm and refuel through their ground crew menu, as on any FARP.
7. **Pack the Fat Cow FARP**, or simply take off: the FARP is packed for you.
   What was not used goes back into your crates, the hold gets lighter, and a crate left empty disappears.

**Fat Cow stock** tells you what you still have, on the ground or in the air.

!!! note "When the stock runs out"
    A helicopter asking for more than what is left gets what is left, and its ground crew answers "Unable to comply".
    A request on an empty FARP gets "We don't have this in stock".

## Asking for an AI cow

Any player whose aircraft is not a cow sees **F10 → CTLD → Fat Cow → Request a Fat Cow**.

1. Land in a friendly logistic zone, and request it there: a cow is never sent to you in the field.
2. A CH-47 starts up 80 m behind you and follows your flight.
3. When your leader lands, the cow lands next to your leader, and opens its FARP one minute later.
4. When your leader takes off, the cow packs and follows again.
5. Once it is empty, or when you use **Release the Fat Cow**, it flies home and disappears.

By default an AI cow carries 7 200 kg of fuel (three 800 gal tanks) and 6 Hellfires.
One cow per flight.

!!! tip "Land in the open"
    The cow lands about 120 m to the left of your leader.
    Pick open ground: a helicopter sent to land at the edge of a forest hovers there and never touches down.
